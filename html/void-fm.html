Void FM.

Broadcasting the sound of the underground.

VOID/FMindependent radio
roleArt direction / web design
year2023
toolsFigma / Web Audio API

the brief

Turn up
the strange.

Void FM is an independent radio experience for curious listeners. It needed to feel raw, immediate, and a little unpolished — like finding a broadcast that was never meant to be found.

A modular grid, electric purple palette, and live frequency-inspired motion give the site its nocturnal energy while keeping the player simple and accessible.
