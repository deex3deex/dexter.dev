Signal Studio.

roleBrand identity / creative development
year2024
toolsFigma / HTML / CSS / JS

the brief

Make some
noise.

Signal Studio is a digital platform for independent artists, makers, and the ideas they put into the world. The brief was to create a site that could feel editorial one moment and electric the next.

I built a flexible visual language around bold type, moving signal bars, and a high-contrast palette so every story gets its own frequency while the whole experience stays unmistakably Signal.
