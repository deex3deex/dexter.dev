Pulse Health.

A calmer way to understand your health data.

pulse.health, reimagined
roleProduct design / frontend
year2024
toolsFigma / HTML / CSS / JS

the brief

Less noise.
More signal.

Pulse turns scattered health metrics into a clear daily rhythm. The goal was to create an interface that feels encouraging instead of clinical, giving people confidence in their small, consistent choices.

I shaped the visual system around soft contrast, generous space, and progressive disclosure — so the important insight always arrives before the overwhelming detail.
