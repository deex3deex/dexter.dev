the brief

Money,
in orbit.

Orbit is a finance dashboard designed to make everyday decisions feel lighter. Instead of burying people in charts, it creates a single visual path from what happened to what to do next.

I built the component system around calm states, clear language, and responsive data visualizations that stay legible from a phone to a desktop command center.
